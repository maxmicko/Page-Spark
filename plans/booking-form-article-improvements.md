```markdown
## 5 Costly Mistakes Mobile Detailers Make with Booking Forms

### Mistake #1: Using Generic Contact Forms
**The Problem:** "Contact us" forms don't capture the information you need.

**What Happens:**
- Customer submits vague request
- You text back asking for details
- They don't respond
- You lose the booking

**The Fix:** Use a form that captures:
- Specific service needed
- Vehicle details
- Preferred date/time
- Exact location

### Mistake #2: Making Forms Too Long
**The Problem:** Asking for too much information upfront.

**The Data:** For every additional field beyond 7, you lose 10% of potential bookings.

**What to Ask:**
✅ Essential: Service, date, location, vehicle, contact
❌ Skip for now: How did you hear about us, marketing preferences, detailed history

**The Fix:** Keep it to 5-7 fields maximum. You can get more details later.

### Mistake #3: No Mobile Optimization
**The Problem:** 94% of detailing bookings start on mobile phones.

**What Happens:**
- Form doesn't work well on phones
- Customers get frustrated
- They book with a competitor instead

**The Fix:** Test your form on your phone BEFORE sharing it. If it's hard for you, it's impossible for customers.

### Mistake #4: No Confirmation or Follow-Up
**The Problem:** Customer submits form and hears nothing.

**The Result:**
- They think it didn't work
- They book elsewhere
- You lose the job

**The Fix:** Automated confirmation messages are non-negotiable. Even a simple "Got it! We'll text you within 2 hours" increases conversion by 34%.

### Mistake #5: Hiding Your Booking Form
**The Problem:** Having a great form that nobody can find.

**Where to Put Your Booking Link:**
✅ Instagram bio (most important!)
✅ Facebook page "Book Now" button
✅ Google Business Profile
✅ Text message signature
✅ Email signature
✅ Vehicle signage QR code

**Pro Tip:** Your booking link should be the FIRST thing people see, not buried in a menu.
```
